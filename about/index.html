About CoTeX

A business built through products, partnerships and trade.

From a family business beginning in Yiwu to an international textile and apparel partner, our story has always been grounded in practical product work.

Our foundation

Rooted in Yiwu. Focused on international business.

CoTeX grew from the experience of a family business that began in Yiwu in 2005. The early years were shaped by direct involvement in product selection, customer communication and the fast-moving trading environment of Yiwu International Trade Market.

Over time, that commercial foundation expanded through close manufacturing relationships and hands-on product development. Today, YIWU COTEX IMPORT & EXPORT CO., LTD. works with international B2B customers across women's and girls' textile and apparel categories.

Our role is to connect market requirements with suitable products and production resources. We coordinate sourcing, development, manufacturing, inspection, export documentation and delivery through a long-standing family manufacturing partner and other specialized partner factories.
