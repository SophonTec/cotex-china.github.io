Product development

Turning market direction into product possibilities.

CoTeX supports the work between an initial idea and a production-ready direction through product selection, design coordination, sampling and visual communication.

Development approach

Designed for clearer decisions.

International product development works best when customer direction, market context and production reality are considered together. We help organize references, color and pattern direction, product combinations and sampling priorities before manufacturing begins.

For textile and apparel projects, our team can coordinate pattern design, panel and layout development, product mockups, sample communication and launch visuals. The objective is not to add complexity, but to make each decision easier to review and execute.

Development support is adapted to the category and project. Final construction, materials and production details are agreed through the inquiry and sampling process rather than assumed from a reference image.
