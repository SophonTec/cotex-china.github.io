ABOUT COTEX

A Family-Run Business
with a Global Vision

Textiles connect people and a better life.

Rooted in a family business that began in Yiwu in 2005, COTEX has grown from its textile trading roots into an international business specializing in women's and girls' products.

Building on our family's manufacturing experience, we work closely with JIHONG and a network of specialized production partners, bringing together product development, manufacturing coordination, quality management and international trade.

OUR FAMILY JOURNEY

Our Story Since 2005

From a textile shop in Yiwu to products for international markets.

  1. 2005

    Our Beginning

    Our family business began with a specialty textile shop in Yiwu, establishing the foundations of our work in fabrics and trade.

  2. 2008

    Business Expansion

    Moving into Yiwu International Trade Market opened new opportunities to connect our products with international customers.

  3. 2015

    Manufacturing Development

    The family expanded into manufacturing, beginning the construction of the JIHONG production facility.

  4. 2017

    Production Expansion

    Expanded production strengthened our textile manufacturing experience and our ability to coordinate customer requirements.

  5. 2023

    Digital Commerce

    Cross-border e-commerce brought new channels for presenting our products and connecting with international markets.

  6. Today

    From Products to Possibilities

    COTEX brings together product development, manufacturing coordination and international trade for women’s and girls’ collections.

These milestones trace our family’s business and manufacturing journey, which forms the foundation of COTEX today.
