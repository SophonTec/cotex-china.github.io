About CoTeX

Product knowledge shaped by real trade and manufacturing.

Rooted in a family business that began in Yiwu in 2005, CoTeX combines textile and apparel product expertise with long-standing manufacturing partnerships, product development and international trade.

Today, we work with overseas B2B customers from product selection and development through production coordination, quality control, export documentation and international delivery. Our model brings together a long-standing family manufacturing partner and a wider network of specialized factories selected according to product needs.
