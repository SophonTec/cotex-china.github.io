Our manufacturing model

A trusted network, managed by CoTeX.

JIHONG is a long-standing family manufacturing partner and an important part of our production network. CoTeX receives and manages overseas orders, then coordinates production with JIHONG or with other qualified specialist factories depending on the product category, process and order requirements.

This model allows the commercial and export team to remain close to the customer while production partners focus on manufacturing. CoTeX follows schedules, product requirements, inspection and shipment preparation throughout the order.

We do not present every product as factory-owned production. Instead, we build the appropriate production route for each project and keep one accountable point of communication for the customer.
